Understand the Global Narrative

CrossLens is an Android news app designed for informed comparison. See how the same event is reported across countries, languages, and institutions. Explore clustered stories, compare source coverage, and read translations while retaining a clear path to the original reporting.

Core Features

We built CrossLens with product principles that put evidence and perspective first.

Global by Design

Organize coverage by source, country, region, language, topic, and institutional context. Geographic, political, and institutional context coexist without universal left/center/right classifications.

Evidence Stays Visible

Link summaries and comparison observations directly to the articles supporting them. Distinctly separate reported claims from established facts so you can verify everything.

Translation with Provenance

Preserve original text alongside labeled translations. We disclose translation availability and methods, ensuring you always have a clear path to the source material.

Gorgeous & Editorial

Inspired by tactile browsing and editorial magazine design. A custom Material 3 visual system with expressive typography, generous imagery, and restrained perspective-switch motion.

The Lens Gap

How differently is this event being covered in the available reporting?

Lens Gap evaluates differences in emphasized claims, selected actors, terminology, and the distribution of attention across sources. Each result reveals its sampled articles, geographic/language coverage, time window, explanatory components, and concrete evidence.


Example: "Lens Gap: 72/100 — divergent emphasis." Not a factuality score, but a measure of reporting divergence.
