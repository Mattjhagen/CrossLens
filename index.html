The CrossLens App V1.0

One event. A world of perspectives.

Events should behave like shared context, not isolated silos. CrossLens is the platform for authoritative news synchronization and comparison across borders.

View Source

Global by Design

Organize coverage by source, country, region, language, topic, and institutional context. Geographic and political context coexist without universal left/center/right classifications.

Evidence Stays Visible

Link summaries and comparison observations directly to the articles supporting them. Distinctly separate reported claims from established facts so you can verify everything.

Translation with Provenance

Preserve original text alongside labeled translations. We disclose translation availability and methods, ensuring you always have a clear path to the source material.

Gorgeous & Editorial

Inspired by tactile browsing and editorial magazine design. A custom Material 3 visual system with expressive typography and restrained perspective-switch motion.

How differently is this event being covered in the available reporting?

Lens Gap evaluates differences in emphasized claims, selected actors, terminology, and the distribution of attention across sources. Each result reveals its sampled articles, geographic/language coverage, time window, explanatory components, and concrete evidence.
